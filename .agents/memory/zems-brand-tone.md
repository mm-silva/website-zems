---
name: Zems brand tone
description: Writing preference for Zems marketing and sales pages.
---

Keep marketing copy concise. Avoid explaining every detail upfront; leave room for prospective clients to discover the offer through a conversation with Z&MS.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for brief copy that lets the client discover more with the team.

**How to apply:** Prefer short headings, clear benefits, and selective detail across Z&MS pages. Do not remove necessary course facts, terms, or contact information.
